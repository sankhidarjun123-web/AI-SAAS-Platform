# Real Mentor AI 🚀
### Your AI-Powered Career Mentor

Real Mentor AI is an AI-powered career development platform designed to help users prepare for interviews, analyze their resumes, improve their professional profiles, and track their career preparation journey.

The platform combines AI-driven mentorship, resume intelligence, mock interviews, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and subscription-based access into a unified experience.

Rather than simply providing generic AI responses, Real Mentor AI aims to deliver personalized career guidance through contextual resume analysis, interactive interview sessions, and detailed performance tracking.

**Status:** Live Deployment — MVP
